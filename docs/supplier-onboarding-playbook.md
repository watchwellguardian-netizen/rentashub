# Supplier Onboarding Playbook

This playbook defines how suppliers enter a controlled RentasHub pilot. It does not replace legal onboarding, real KYC, insurance validation, payment setup, or production approval.

## Supplier Invitation

1. Confirm supplier fits the pilot region and selected asset categories.
2. Send invitation with pilot scope, support channel, operating hours, and non-live provider disclosure.
3. Explain that payments, escrow, insurance, KYC, and object storage may still be simulated or credential-ready only.
4. Record invitation status and owner.

## Business Profile Setup

Supplier must complete:

- Business name.
- Contact person.
- Phone.
- Email.
- Business address.
- Service areas.
- Supplier type.
- Bio/description.
- Business hours.
- Emergency contact.
- Logo/photo placeholder if uploads are not active.

## Asset Listing Standards

Each asset listing should include:

- Clear title.
- Correct category and subcategory.
- Accurate location.
- Rental/sale/trade/swap availability where applicable.
- Availability status.
- Price/rate or sale/trade value where applicable.
- Deposit requirement.
- Delivery/pickup options.
- Damage, cancellation, safety, and usage instructions.
- Operator requirement.
- Verification status.

## Photo Requirements

When object storage is activated, request:

- Front, rear, and side photos.
- Interior/control-panel photos where relevant.
- Serial/VIN/plate placeholders handled privately.
- Damage/wear closeups.
- Accessories and attachments.
- Safety labels or compliance plates when relevant.

Until storage is active, collect metadata only and do not request private documents through unapproved channels.

## Verification Checklist

Collect placeholders or approved private upload records for:

- ID document.
- Business registration.
- Proof of address.
- Insurance document.
- Asset ownership proof.
- Operator certification where relevant.

Document review is not real legal/KYC verification until a provider and policy are active.

## Trust Score Explanation

Explain that trust score may consider:

- Verification completeness.
- Listing quality.
- Booking completion.
- Response behavior.
- Reviews.
- Disputes.
- Cancellation history.
- Inspection history.

Trust score is a marketplace signal, not a legal certification.

## Booking Response Expectations

Pilot suppliers should:

- Respond to booking requests during operating hours.
- Keep availability updated.
- Use messages for booking clarification.
- Complete inspection/check-in/check-out steps where applicable.
- Escalate disputes or safety issues immediately.

## Dispute and Claim Process

Suppliers must preserve:

- Booking ID.
- Inspection record.
- Photos/evidence metadata.
- Message thread.
- Damage notes.
- Timeline.

Disputes and claims are operational placeholders unless legal, insurance, escrow, and payment workflows are activated.

## Support Contact Rules

- Routine support: use `PILOT_SUPPORT_EMAIL`.
- Urgent escalation: use `PILOT_ESCALATION_EMAIL`.
- Do not send private KYC documents over unapproved channels.
- Do not promise payouts, escrow release, or insurance decisions during credential-ready mode.
