# RentasHub Brand Governance

Official brand governance v1.0.

## Brand Name

RentasHub

## Official Tagline

Rent. Buy. Sell. Trade.

## Brand Positioning

RentasHub is a unified marketplace where individuals and businesses can rent, buy, sell, trade, broker, and manage vehicles, equipment, property, tools, event spaces, storage assets, and specialty assets from one trusted platform.

## Logo System

### Version A - Master Brand Logo

Default platform logo. Structure:

- R icon.
- RentasHub wordmark.
- Rent. Buy. Sell. Trade. tagline.

Use on website headers, dashboards, login, registration, mobile/PWA surfaces, email templates, contracts, invoices, verification screens, notifications, reports, documents, and investor materials.

### Version B - Marketplace Hero Logo

Marketing-only logo. Structure:

- Modern SUV.
- Semi truck.
- Excavator.
- R icon.
- RentasHub wordmark.
- Rent. Buy. Sell. Trade. tagline.

Use only for marketing surfaces such as homepage hero sections, landing pages, brochures, trade shows, campaigns, investor presentations, press kits, and advertising.

Not permitted in dashboard headers, navigation bars, login pages, invoices, or mobile app headers.

### Version C - App Icon

R icon only. No text, tagline, vehicles, or equipment icons.

Use for favicon, browser tabs, Android/iOS app icon, desktop shortcut, social profile image, and app launcher.

## Logo Rules

Do use approved colors, typography, spacing, and proportions.

Do not add vehicles to the master logo, add equipment icons to the master logo, add shadows, stretch, rotate, recolor, or change the tagline.

## Typography

Primary font: Sora SemiBold.

Secondary font: Sora Regular.

Fallbacks: Manrope, Montserrat, Outfit, system sans-serif.

Do not use comic, script, decorative, condensed, or novelty fonts.

## Color System

- Primary Blue: `#0A4DA3`
- Marketplace Orange: `#F58220`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Dark Navy: `#0B1F3A`

## Consistency Rules

Every screen must use RentasHub branding, approved color palette, approved typography, consistent button styles, consistent spacing, and consistent iconography.

No legacy branding.

No PlannasHub references.

No RentBroker references.

No parent-product branding, mixed logos, or alternate color systems are allowed in user-facing source.
